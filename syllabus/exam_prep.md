## Part 1 — CSIR-UGC-DBT-NET Life Sciences (JRF)

### Unit 1 — Structure and Function of Biomolecules
- [ ] Atoms, molecules, chemical bonds
- [ ] Biomolecule composition/structure/function — carbohydrates, lipids, proteins, nucleic acids, vitamins
- [ ] Stabilizing interactions — van der Waals, electrostatic, H-bonding, hydrophobic
- [ ] Biophysical chemistry — pH, buffers, reaction kinetics, thermodynamics, colligative properties
- [ ] Bioenergetics — glycolysis, oxidative phosphorylation, coupled reactions, energy transducers
- [ ] Enzyme catalysis and kinetics — regulation, mechanism, isozymes
- [ ] Protein conformation — Ramachandran plot, secondary structure, domains, motifs, folds
- [ ] Nucleic acid conformation — A/B/Z DNA, RNA structure
- [ ] Stability of proteins and nucleic acids
- [ ] Metabolism — carbohydrates, lipids, amino acids, nucleotides, vitamins

### Unit 2 — Cellular Organization
- [ ] Cell wall and cell membrane structure/function
- [ ] Membrane transport — diffusion, osmosis, ion channels, active transport, pumps
- [ ] Intracellular organelles — nucleus, mitochondria, Golgi, lysosomes, ER, peroxisomes, plastids, vacuoles, chloroplast
- [ ] Cytoskeleton structure/function and role in motility
- [ ] Gene and chromosome organization — operons, repetitive DNA, gene families, chromatin, transposons
- [ ] Cell division and cell cycle — mitosis, meiosis, regulation, apoptosis, necrosis, autophagy
- [ ] Microbial physiology — growth kinetics, stress response, antimicrobial resistance

### Unit 3 — Fundamental Processes
- [ ] DNA replication, repair and recombination
- [ ] RNA synthesis, processing and regulation — transcription machinery, splicing, ncRNA, riboswitches
- [ ] Protein synthesis, processing and degradation — ribosomes, translation, PTMs, trafficking
- [ ] Control of gene expression — transcriptional/translational regulation, chromatin, epigenetics

### Unit 4 — Cell Communication and Cell Signaling
- [ ] Cell signaling — hormones, receptors, GPCRs, second messengers, two-component systems
- [ ] Cellular communication — adhesion molecules, gap junctions, ECM, integrins, neurotransmission
- [ ] Innate and adaptive immune system — antigens, antibodies, MHC, B/T cell activation, vaccines
- [ ] Host-pathogen interaction — recognition, entry, host manipulation by pathogens
- [ ] Cancer biology — oncogenes, tumor suppressors, metastasis, therapeutic interventions

### Unit 5 — Developmental Biology
- [ ] Basic concepts — potency, commitment, determination, differentiation, stem cells
- [ ] Gametogenesis, fertilization, early development (animal and plant)
- [ ] Morphogenesis/organogenesis in animals — Dictyostelium, Drosophila, C. elegans, vertebrate limb/eye
- [ ] Morphogenesis/organogenesis in plants — meristems, leaf development, flowering transition
- [ ] Programmed cell death, aging and senescence

### Unit 6 — System Physiology: Plant
- [ ] Photosynthesis — light harvesting, electron transport, C3/C4/CAM
- [ ] Respiration and photorespiration
- [ ] Nitrogen metabolism — nitrate/ammonium assimilation, biological N fixation
- [ ] Plant hormones — biosynthesis, transport, mechanism of action
- [ ] Sensory photobiology — phytochromes, cryptochromes, phototropins, photoperiodism
- [ ] Solute transport and photoassimilate translocation
- [ ] Secondary metabolites — terpenes, phenolics, alkaloids
- [ ] Stress physiology — biotic/abiotic responses, innate immunity

### Unit 7 — System Physiology: Animal
- [ ] Blood and circulation
- [ ] Cardiovascular system
- [ ] Respiratory system
- [ ] Nervous system
- [ ] Sense organs — vision, hearing, tactile
- [ ] Excretory system
- [ ] Thermoregulation
- [ ] Stress and adaptation
- [ ] Digestive system
- [ ] Endocrinology and reproduction
- [ ] Gut microbiome / holobionts — gut-brain axis, dysbiosis
- [ ] Interorgan communication and energy homeostasis

### Unit 8 — Inheritance Biology
- [ ] Chromosomal and extrachromosomal inheritance — Mendelian, linkage, maternal inheritance
- [ ] Genes and mutations — alleles, mutation types, complementation
- [ ] Genetic analysis — linkage maps, molecular markers, gene transfer in bacteria
- [ ] Human genetics — pedigree analysis, karyotypes, genetic disorders
- [ ] Quantitative genetics — Hardy-Weinberg, heritability, polygenic inheritance
- [ ] Chromosomal structural/numerical alterations — deletion, duplication, inversion, ploidy

### Unit 9 — Evolution and Diversity of Life Forms
- [ ] Evolution of life and life forms — origin of life, mechanisms, speciation, human evolution
- [ ] Principles and methods of taxonomy
- [ ] Microbial life — bacteria, archaea, viruses
- [ ] Protists — algae, protozoa, slime molds
- [ ] Fungi — chytrids to basidiomycetes, plant/human pathogens
- [ ] Plant life — bryophytes to angiosperms
- [ ] Animal life — invertebrates and vertebrates

### Unit 10 — Ecology and Behavioural Biology
- [ ] Introduction to ecology — levels of organization, adaptations
- [ ] Population ecology — growth models, life tables, survivorship
- [ ] Community ecology — food webs, species interactions, succession
- [ ] Ecosystem ecology — energy flow, biogeochemical cycles
- [ ] Human impacts on ecosystems — land use, climate change, pollution
- [ ] Biodiversity and conservation — IUCN categories, PVA, conservation strategies
- [ ] Acts and policies — Biodiversity Act 2002, PGRFA, PPVFR
- [ ] Behavioural ecology — foraging, communication, mating systems

### Unit 11 — Bioinformatics and Computational Biology
- [ ] Major bioinformatic resources/databases
- [ ] Sequence analysis basics — BLAST, FASTA, homology, MSA
- [ ] Gene annotation and phylogenetics
- [ ] Molecular modelling and dynamics
- [ ] Protein 3D structure classification/prediction — homology modeling, AlphaFold
- [ ] Drug design — receptor-ligand interactions, QSAR, ADMET
- [ ] Systems biology — data science in biology, disease modeling

### Unit 12 — Biochemical Engineering and Industrial Biotechnology
- [ ] Introductory mathematics — calculus, ODEs, linear algebra, numerical methods
- [ ] Engineering principles — material/energy balance, transport phenomena
- [ ] Thermodynamics in biological systems
- [ ] Bioprocess engineering — growth kinetics, bioreactors, fermentation technology
- [ ] Enzymes and microbial technology — biocatalysis, immobilization
- [ ] Downstream processing — extraction, chromatography, drying
- [ ] Bioprocess plant design and scale-up
- [ ] Metabolic engineering and synthetic biology

### Unit 13 — Advances in Biotechnology
- [ ] Recombinant DNA technology — cloning, expression, genome editing
- [ ] Medical biotechnology — vaccines, stem cells, immunotherapy, organoids
- [ ] Animal biotechnology — transgenics, cloning, tissue culture
- [ ] Agriculture biotechnology — transgenic plants, marker-assisted breeding
- [ ] Marine biotechnology — marine organisms, resources, microbes, pharmacology
- [ ] Environmental biotechnology — bioremediation, biofuels, waste management

### Unit 14 — Methods in Biology
- [ ] Molecular biology techniques — chromatography, electrophoresis, centrifugation
- [ ] Biophysical methods — spectroscopy, X-ray, cryo-EM, mass spec
- [ ] Genomics/transcriptomics/proteomics/metabolomics — comparative genomics, single-cell omics
- [ ] Radiolabeling techniques
- [ ] Histochemical and immunotechniques — ELISA, western blot, flow cytometry, FISH
- [ ] Microscopic techniques
- [ ] Electrophysiological methods — patch-clamp, ECG, fMRI
- [ ] Methods in field biology
- [ ] Statistical methods — regression, t-test, ANOVA, chi-square
- [ ] IPR, biosafety and bioethics — patents, biosafety levels, research ethics, AI in research

## Part 2 — GATE Biotechnology (BT)

### Section 1 — Engineering Mathematics
- [ ] Linear algebra — matrices, determinants, systems of equations, eigenvalues/eigenvectors
- [ ] Calculus — multivariable, partial derivatives, maxima/minima, sequences & series, convergence, gradient/divergence/curl
- [ ] Differential equations — linear/nonlinear ODEs, Cauchy's/Euler's equations, Laplace transforms
- [ ] Probability and statistics — distributions, correlation/regression, Bayesian statistics
- [ ] Numerical methods — root finding, integration (trapezoidal/Simpson), single-step ODE solvers

### Section 2 — General Biology
- [ ] Biochemistry — biomolecules, membranes, metabolism regulation, enzyme kinetics/inhibition
- [ ] Microbiology — classification, cell wall, growth/nutrition, operons, host-pathogen, antibiotics
- [ ] Immunology — lymphoid organs, innate/adaptive immunity, antibody diversity, MHC, vaccines

### Section 3 — Genetics, Cellular and Molecular Biology
- [ ] Genetics and evolutionary biology — Mendelian inheritance, linkage, microbial genetics, population genetics, epigenetics
- [ ] Cell biology — eukaryotic cell structure, cell cycle, signaling, PTMs, protein trafficking, ECM

### Section 4 — Fundamentals of Biological Engineering
- [ ] Bioreaction engineering — rate laws, ideal reactors, immobilization, scale-up
- [ ] Upstream/downstream processing — sterilization, filtration, chromatography, process control

### Section 5 — Plant, Animal and Microbial Biotechnology
- [ ] Plant biotechnology — totipotency, tissue culture, transgenics, protoplast fusion
- [ ] Animal biotechnology — cell culture, hybridoma, stem cells, transgenic/knockout animals
- [ ] Microbial biotechnology — biomass/metabolite production, recombinant protein production

### Section 6 — Recombinant DNA Technology and Other Tools
- [ ] Recombinant DNA technology — enzymes, vectors, cloning strategies, gene targeting
- [ ] Molecular tools — PCR, sequencing, blotting, CRISPR-Cas, biosensors
- [ ] Analytical tools — microscopy, spectroscopy, electrophoresis, immunoassays, flow cytometry
- [ ] Computational tools — bioinformatics resources, sequence/structure analysis, metagenomics

## Part 3 — GATE Life Sciences (XL)

> XL requires XL0 (compulsory) plus 2 optional subjects. Given a marine
> biotech / molecular biology background, XL1 (Biochemistry) and XL3
> (Microbiology) are the natural picks — adjust if you decide otherwise.
> Only the 2 you actually choose need to reach 100%; the rest are here
> for reference/overlap with NET-JRF prep.

### XL0 — Chemistry (compulsory for all XL candidates)
- [ ] Atomic structure and periodicity
- [ ] Structure and bonding — VSEPR, hybridization, hydrogen bonding
- [ ] s, p and d block elements — coordination complexes
- [ ] Bioinorganic chemistry — metalloproteins, carbonic anhydrase, SOD
- [ ] Chemical equilibria — osmotic pressure, pH, buffers
- [ ] Electrochemistry — Nernst equation, cell potentials
- [ ] Reaction kinetics — enzyme kinetics, inhibition
- [ ] Thermodynamics — laws, entropy, free energy
- [ ] Structure-reactivity correlations and organic reaction mechanisms
- [ ] Chemistry of biomolecules — amino acids, DNA sequencing, chromatography

### XL1 — Biochemistry (recommended pick)
- [ ] Structure and function of biomolecules — myoglobin, hemoglobin, lysozyme
- [ ] Enzymes and general principles of metabolism — kinetics, coenzymes, ATP
- [ ] Metabolic pathways and associated disorders — glycolysis, TCA, gluconeogenesis
- [ ] Analytical techniques — chromatography, electrophoresis, spectroscopy, NGS
- [ ] Cell structure and function — membranes, signalling, receptors
- [ ] Gene expression regulation and DNA repair

### XL2 — Botany (optional)
- [ ] Plant systematics — nomenclature, classification, molecular taxonomy
- [ ] Plant anatomy — root/stem/leaf, vascular system
- [ ] Plant development, cell and tissue morphogenesis
- [ ] Plant physiology and biochemistry — water relations, photosynthesis, hormones
- [ ] Genetics and genomics — inheritance, epigenetics, gene silencing
- [ ] Plant breeding, genetic modification, genome editing — CRISPR/Cas9, tissue culture
- [ ] Economic and applied botany
- [ ] Plant pathology — diseases, resistance mechanisms
- [ ] Ecology and environment

### XL3 — Microbiology (recommended pick)
- [ ] Historical perspective
- [ ] Methods in microbiology — pure culture, sterilization, PCR, CRISPR-Cas
- [ ] Microbial taxonomy and diversity
- [ ] Prokaryotic cell structure and function
- [ ] Microbial growth — growth curve, synchronous/continuous culture
- [ ] Microbial metabolism — energetics, fermentation, photosynthesis
- [ ] Microbial diseases and host-pathogen interaction
- [ ] Control of microorganisms — antibiotics, antifungals, antivirals
- [ ] Microbial genetics — mutation, transformation, conjugation, transduction
- [ ] Microbial ecology — biogeochemical cycles, metagenomics

### XL4 — Zoology (optional)
- [ ] Animal diversity and phylogenetics
- [ ] Evolution
- [ ] Genetics — inheritance, population genetics, model organisms
- [ ] Biochemistry and molecular biology
- [ ] Cell biology
- [ ] Gene expression in eukaryotes
- [ ] Animal anatomy and physiology
- [ ] Parasitology and immunology
- [ ] Developmental biology
- [ ] Ecology
- [ ] Animal behaviour

### XL5 — Food Technology (optional, likely skip)
- [ ] Food chemistry and nutrition
- [ ] Food microbiology
- [ ] Food products technology
- [ ] Food engineering
